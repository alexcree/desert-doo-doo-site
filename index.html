FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Just make sure we can access the yard — same as your pool cleaner. Dogs stay secured indoors by default, and our cleaner can step away anytime if a situation feels unsafe.

Where does the waste go?

Bagged and placed in your own bin. We bring the bags and the deodorizing treatment.

What if I have more than two dogs?

The flat rate covers two dogs; each additional dog is $5 per visit.

Can I pause for the summer?

Yes — snowbird seasonal holds pause your billing and automatically resume in the fall. No cancellation needed.

What if I'm not happy with a visit?

Send us the photos and we'll adjudicate — if we missed a spot, we re-clean free within 24 hours.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, effective immediately. Text the concierge or hit cancel in your account — no phone maze, no retention script.
